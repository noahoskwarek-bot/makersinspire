Our Story

Heirloom meets a night out.

Makers Inspire is one-of-a-kind artisan jewelry by Erika Fry: mostly sterling silver and gold-filled, with gemstone beads and other stones, and now a little copper and stainless steel.

The pieces are made for women who want to feel even more beautiful. Each one is unique and becomes yours, because jewelry carries emotion and memory. The look is sentimental and timeless, and also glamorous and fun.
